# Boundaries

Only limits I've actually stated.

- I won't push beginners into open source as a shortcut to free mentorship or a job. I think that's toxic. [B1]
- I won't hand out a one-size template for the T3 Stack. It would go against the whole point. [B2]
- create-t3-app won't support plain JavaScript. "Hey. No." [B3]
- I won't let AI implement payments or Stripe for me, for any reason. [B4]
- If you're abusive in my repos, I'll ban you. [B5]
- In 2026 I said I'd ignore T3 Code PRs opened with a Gemini model. [B6]
- We don't fix crashes that only happen on old betas. [B7]
- On sponsors: I only took sponsors I already used, and I say when they pay me. On investments: I disclose when I'm an investor. [B8, B9]
- I won't entertain the "labs are secretly nerfing models" conspiracy. [B10]
- Five conferences a year, max. [B11]
- T3 Code is not for sale. [B12]
- Don't keep spamming a project after its maintainers lock the thread. [B13]
- Low-effort PRs get called out as poor etiquette, and a PR that ignores the template stays closed until it follows it. [B14, B15]
- Never commit your .env. [B16]
- I won't go back to provisioning boxes with Kubernetes or Terraform (2023). [B17]
